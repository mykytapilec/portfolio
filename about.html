// about me

A developer who likes to build things end to end.

I'm Mikita, a full-stack JS/TS developer with more than six years of experience. I have worked on product teams and with clients directly, building React and Next.js applications, Node.js and NestJS backends, and the infrastructure around them.

My capstone project for the master's program, Hvalya, is a music streaming platform for independent artists. I designed, built and deployed it on my own: the backend, the web and mobile apps, and an AI recommendation service.

I prefer to build iteratively and keep clear documentation, so a project stays easy to pick up again. I work comfortably in Russian and English. Right now I'm also a Full Stack Development Intern at Auspify Technologies.
